**Decision.** Superhuman asked users one question: how would you feel if you could no longer use the product? The team tracked the share of "very disappointed" answers as its main metric. The target was 40%, a benchmark Sean Ellis found across about 100 startups. The team then narrowed to the personas who answered "very disappointed", such as founders, managers, and executives. It split the roadmap in half. One half deepened what those users loved, such as speed and shortcuts. The other half removed what held back near-fans, such as the missing mobile app and integrations.

**Outcome.** The score was 22% in summer 2017. Segmenting to the core personas raised it to 33%. After three quarters of roadmap work it reached 58%.

**Lesson.** Measure product-market fit with one repeatable number, and build first for the segment that already loves the product.
