**Decision.** Tract tried to speed up UK planning permission with software. It raised £744,000 in April 2024 and tried four models in turn. The last was Tract Editor, an LLM tool that drafted planning documents. The founders say they put too much into technology and too little into business development.

**Outcome.** Design partners praised the drafts. When offered a 50% discount, they still asked for more features before they would sign. The product had to replace an existing workflow plus habit, and being better was not enough. After nearly two years the company had zero revenue. In March 2025 it closed and returned the remaining capital.

**Lesson.** Positive feedback on an AI demo is not a purchase, so ask for a commitment early and compare against the workflow the buyer already has.
