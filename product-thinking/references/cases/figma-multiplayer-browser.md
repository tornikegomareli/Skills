**Decision.** Figma's early model kept files local. In teams this caused overwritten work and version conflicts. The team spent a year building real-time multiplayer editing in the browser, so several people edit the same file at once. It shipped with live cursors, presence avatars, and a presentation mode. Co-founder Evan Wallace announced it at the public launch in September 2016.

**Outcome.** Multiplayer became Figma's core feature. Adobe agreed to buy Figma for about $20B in September 2022. The deal was dropped in December 2023 over regulators, and Adobe paid a $1B break fee. Figma went public in July 2025 and closed its first day at a $56.3B market value. It reported 13M users in 2025.

**Lesson.** Design for the whole team's job, not one person's file, and the product removes the steps that break between people.
