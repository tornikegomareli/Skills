**Decision.** Humane raised over $200M and launched a $699 AI wearable with a $24 monthly subscription. Early testers, including friends, investors, and staff, called it frustrating. The company launched it anyway.

**Outcome.** Reviews in April 2024 were very negative. The Verge found it could not set an alarm, a timer, or a calendar entry. Queries took about 10 seconds and often ended in a generic error. It overheated and drained its batteries in hours. Humane hoped to ship about 100,000 Pins in the first year but shipped about 10,000. From May to August 2024, returns were higher than sales. In February 2025 HP bought most of the company for $116M, and the Pin was shut off.

**Lesson.** A new device must first do the basic jobs users expect from any assistant, and warnings from early testers are data, not noise.
