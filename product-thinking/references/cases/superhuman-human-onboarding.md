**Decision.** Superhuman required a 1:1 onboarding call before a new customer could use the email app. The buyers were busy founders and executives, Gmail was always the easy fallback, and the price was $30 a month. Early calls took 90 minutes: 30 for the user's workflow and pain, 60 to set up the product. Later calls took 30 minutes. A user's June 2020 post shows the calls were still mandatory then. (The source does not give the start year. The `year` field is the latest date we could confirm.)

**Outcome.** Human-led onboarding gave 2x the activation rate and 2x the referrals of self-serve. 65% of customers onboarded by hand moved fully to Superhuman. Each fully ramped specialist brought about $650k ARR a year, at a cost of $60k to $130k each. The cost was friction: about 15% of users missed their call, and some gave up on scheduling. Superhuman later moved most onboarding to self-serve and kept human calls for the highest-value B2B accounts.

**Lesson.** For a complex, high-priced product, a human first session can buy activation that the product cannot yet deliver alone, so measure activation and move the lessons into the product.
