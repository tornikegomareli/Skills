**Decision.** Juicero sold a Wi-Fi juice press for $699 that only worked with its own $5 to $7 produce packs. The press checked a code on each pack and refused other packs.

**Outcome.** Sales were slow. The price dropped to $399, and the company planned a second machine at about $200. In April 2017 Bloomberg showed that people could squeeze the packs by hand. In July Juicero laid off about a quarter of its staff. It said it could not cut costs enough as a standalone company. It stopped all sales and shut down on 1 September 2017, 16 months after launch.

**Lesson.** Complex hardware does not create value if the core job works without it and the price is far above what buyers will pay.
