**Decision.** Quibi raised $1.75B and spent over $1B on short shows for phones before it tested demand. It launched as a phone-only app. At launch, users could not take screenshots to share clips, and they could not cast shows to a TV.

**Outcome.** Quibi launched on 6 April 2020. A week later it fell out of the top 50 free iPhone apps. It added sharing and TV casting only after launch. In June 2020 it was on track for about 2 million first-year subscribers against a target of 7.4 million. It had about 500,000 subscribers when it announced the shutdown on 21 October 2020. It closed around 1 December 2020 and sold its library to Roku for less than $100M.

**Lesson.** A large budget does not prove demand, and a product that blocks sharing removes the main way new users find it.
