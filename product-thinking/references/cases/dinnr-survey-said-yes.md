**Decision.** Dinnr delivered pre-measured recipe ingredients in London on the same day. The founder validated it by pitching the idea and asking people if they would buy. In a survey, 70% of 250 target customers said yes. In interviews, 7 of 10 people liked it. He also copied a model that worked in Scandinavia without new research. He set monthly goals but no rule for what to do when they were missed.

**Outcome.** Dinnr got 3 orders on the first day and 12 in the first week in September 2012. Only 2 of the 10 interviewees became customers. A redesigned website brought a short spike, then the repurchase rate fell back. After 15 months it had about one order per day at about £26 each, and lost money on every order. The founder closed it on 12 January 2014.

**Lesson.** Compliments and survey answers about future buying are not demand, so ask about past behaviour and for a real commitment.
