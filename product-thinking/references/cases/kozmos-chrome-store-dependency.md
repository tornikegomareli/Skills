**Decision.** Kozmos was a paid bookmark and new-tab service. It reached users only through a Chrome extension, so the Chrome Web Store controlled its distribution.

**Outcome.** For about two years, automated reviews sent repeated takedown notices, and the founder answered them by hand. A few months before May 2020, Google removed the extension with no notice and no clear reason. The founder had no support contact and no other channel. He refunded customers and shut the service down.

**Lesson.** If one platform controls your only distribution channel, plan for its removal before it happens.
