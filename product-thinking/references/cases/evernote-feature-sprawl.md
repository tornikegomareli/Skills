**Decision.** Evernote spread past its core note app. It shipped separate apps such as Food (2011), Hello, Peek, Skitch, and Clearly, plus apps for many platforms. In January 2014, Jason Kincaid wrote that the core app had serious bugs. A long audio note of his became unplayable, and the Mac helper app wrote full note text into its log files. He said the company had spread itself too thin across platforms instead of making the core reliable. The post reached 451 points on Hacker News. CEO Phil Libin replied publicly that Kincaid was right and that 2014 would focus on the core product.

**Outcome.** In July 2015 Chris O'Neill replaced Libin as CEO. Evernote then cut the side apps. Food stopped on September 30, 2015. Hello and Peek also closed. Skitch (except Mac), Clearly, and the Pebble app left the stores after January 22, 2016. The company said most of their features were already in the main app. That year it laid off 18% of staff and closed 3 of its 10 offices. The core service still had about 150 million registered users.

**Lesson.** Each new app adds a surface to maintain, so check whether the core still works before you ship the next one.
