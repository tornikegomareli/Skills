**Decision.** Melanie Perkins taught design programs to students at the University of Western Australia. She saw them spend a long time finding buttons before they could design anything. With Cliff Obrecht she first built Fusion Books, an online yearbook design tool for schools. They co-founded Canva in 2013 with Cameron Adams. Its goal was to let people with no design training make good designs, with drag and drop and ready-made templates.

**Outcome.** Canva had more than 750,000 users in its first year and was profitable by 2017. By 2020 users had made over 3 billion designs, and 90,000 schools and universities used it. At the end of 2025 Canva reported more than 265 million monthly active users and US$4 billion in annual recurring revenue.

**Lesson.** Measure the product by what non-experts can now make, and give them good examples to start from.
