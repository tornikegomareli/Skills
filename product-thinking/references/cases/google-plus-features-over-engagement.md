**Decision.** Google launched Google+ in June 2011. It was Google's fourth social network after Buzz, Friend Connect, and Orkut. The team shipped a long list of features: Circles, Hangouts, Sparks, Huddle, Pages, Communities, Events, and photo storage. Google reported account growth: 90 million users by the end of 2011 and 540 million monthly actives by 2013.

**Outcome.** Usage did not follow the account numbers. In January 2012, users spent about 3.3 minutes a month on Google+, against 7.5 hours on Facebook. Vic Gundotra, the project lead, left in April 2014. In 2015 Bradley Horowitz split Photos and communications out as separate services. In October 2018 Google said 90% of Google+ sessions lasted under five seconds. It announced the end of the consumer product in the same post as an API bug that exposed profile data of up to 500,000 accounts. The consumer service closed in April 2019.

**Lesson.** A list of shipped features and a count of sign-ups say nothing about whether people use the product, so measure time and return visits from the first release.
