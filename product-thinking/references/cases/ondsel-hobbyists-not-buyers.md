**Decision.** Ondsel raised venture money to build a commercial business on the open-source CAD tool FreeCAD. It shipped its own FreeCAD edition with a better interface and a cloud service for teams. It competed against closed-source CAD that schools teach and industry already uses.

**Outcome.** The team surveyed users and interviewed nearly a hundred engineers, makers, and shop owners. Hobbyists and independent users supported it, but commercial customers did not adopt it at a level that justified venture funding. After almost two years it found no product-market fit, ran out of runway, and closed in late 2024. The team had 145 pull requests merged into FreeCAD after its May 2024 release, and that work went into FreeCAD 1.0.

**Lesson.** Goodwill from free users is not a buying signal from the customers who must fund a venture-scale business.
