**Decision.** Bill Nguyen and Peter Pham founded Color Labs. It raised $41 million before launch, including $25 million from Sequoia. The iOS app launched on March 24, 2011. It showed photos taken by people near you, in an "elastic" network of strangers. Sequoia invested three days before the launch.

**Outcome.** The app felt empty to anyone with no other users nearby. Reviews were poor and users found its purpose unclear. About 1 million people downloaded it, but active users fell below 100,000 by September 2011. Pham left in June 2011. Nguyen told the New York Times the company would go back to the drawing board. Sequoia's Doug Leone said the big launch had little to show. In October 2012 the board voted to shut down. Apple hired the staff for about $7 million, and the app closed at the end of 2012.

**Lesson.** A product that works only when many strangers nearby adopt it at once needs a plan for the empty first day, and funding does not replace that plan.
