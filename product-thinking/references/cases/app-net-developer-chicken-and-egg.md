**Decision.** App.net was a paid social platform. It counted on third-party developers to build apps that would bring in and keep users. The team knew this was a risk and took it anyway. In May 2014 it moved the service to maintenance mode to save money.

**Outcome.** Developer interest at the start was higher than expected. That interest did not turn into enough paying users for those developers. Revenue fell steadily for more than two years, and the team could not restart active work. App.net shut down on 14 March 2017.

**Lesson.** A platform that depends on partners to create user demand needs its own plan to bring users.
