**Decision.** By mid-2018, Duolingo's daily active users grew at a single-digit rate per year. The product team built a growth model. It split users into states such as new, current, reactivated, resurrected, at-risk, and dormant. Then it tested which transition rate moved DAU most. Current user retention rate (CURR), the share of today's active users who return tomorrow, had 5 times the effect of the next best rate. The team made CURR its focus and shipped leaderboards, streak changes, and many push notification tests.

**Outcome.** Leaderboards raised learning time by 17% and tripled the number of highly engaged users. CURR rose by 21%, which cut daily churn of current users by more than 40%. DAU grew 4.5x over four years, up to the 2021 IPO.

**Lesson.** Model where your active users come from and leave to, then put the team on the one retention rate that moves the total most.
