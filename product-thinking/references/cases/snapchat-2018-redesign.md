**Decision.** Snap announced a redesign in November 2017. It put friends' Stories and incoming snaps on one page, and the Discover section gained sponsored content. Sending snaps and rewatching Stories got harder. Users said Stories mixed with chats were hard to find. Snap said the goal was to put everything from friends in one place.

**Outcome.** A Change.org petition to reverse the redesign passed 1.2 million signatures. In February 2018 a critical tweet from Kylie Jenner was reported to cut more than $1.3 billion from Snap's market value. On February 21, Snap said it would not go back, and it added tabs to sort Stories, group chats, and subscriptions. In Q2 2018, daily active users fell from 191 million to 188 million, the first decline since launch. Evan Spiegel blamed lower usage on the disruption from the redesign.

**Lesson.** If a redesign moves what daily users rely on, test it with them before full rollout, because the cost shows up in active users.
