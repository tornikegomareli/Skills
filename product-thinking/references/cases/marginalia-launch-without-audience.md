**Decision.** The founder turned a personal email-to-notes tool into a paid journal for programmers. Friends encouraged him, so he added billing and a $5 a month plan. He launched with one blog post that reached the Hacker News front page, and he had no audience before that.

**Outcome.** A few people signed up and never came back. Developers did not want to pay when their editor did the job, and many did not want to keep a journal on an outside service. Hosting cost $29 a month for more than a year with zero income, and paid ads brought no return. When he shut it down, it had one active user.

**Lesson.** Your own itch is not a market, so confirm interest and willingness to pay before you build billing.
