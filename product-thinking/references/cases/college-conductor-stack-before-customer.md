**Decision.** A solo developer built a SaaS tool for independent college consultants, with his wife as the first customer. He chose a stack that was new to him (Ember and Semantic UI) plus about 15 other services. He spent many evenings on package upgrades. He did no customer development and showed his wife nothing usable for a long time.

**Outcome.** He worked with Ember for nearly two years. When he replaced it with server-rendered Django, he rebuilt all the original features in two weeks. By then his wife, the only customer voice, had lost interest. After three years the service had no real customers, and he shut it down in 2019.

**Lesson.** Put the simplest useful version in front of the first customer fast, using tools you already know.
