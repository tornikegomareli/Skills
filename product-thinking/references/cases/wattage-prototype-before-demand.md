**Decision.** Wattage wanted to let anyone design custom electronics in a browser. Being a hardware company, it spent its first year on prototypes that proved the idea was technically possible. It planned a whole platform for hardware creation. It tried to raise a $2M seed round before launch.

**Outcome.** The team could not show traction, so investors would not fund an unproven market. The founder later said they should have shipped something much lighter to test interest, and sold one customizable product. They chose to focus on a radio, but too late. Of 1,600 newsletter sign-ups, many wanted things that current technology could not build. After a year of work the company ran out of cash and shut down.

**Lesson.** Proving that you can build something does not prove that people want it.
