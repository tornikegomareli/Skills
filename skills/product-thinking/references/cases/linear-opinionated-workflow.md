**Decision.** Linear built an issue tracker with a fixed, opinionated workflow and published its rules as the Linear Method. The method says flexible tools let each team invent a workflow, and that leads to chaos as teams grow. Other rules: build for the people who do the work, not for reports. Be simple to start and grow more powerful with scale. Use plain names, so a project is called a project. Work in steady cycles, not sprints.

**Outcome.** Linear has been profitable since 2021, a year after launch. In September 2023 it raised a $35M Series B. It had a negative lifetime burn: more cash in the bank than it ever raised. It had 50 employees, and customers included Cash App, Vercel, Retool, and Ramp.

**Lesson.** Choose one good workflow for your users instead of making them design their own.
