**Decision.** Homejoy, a home-cleaning marketplace, chose growth first. It sold $19.99 first cleanings on Groupon and cut prices to enter new cities. It expanded to more than 30 cities in the US, Canada, and Europe, and grew from about 20 to over 100 staff in two years.

**Outcome.** Internal data showed most deal buyers never booked again. A third-party analysis found that only about a quarter of customers stayed after one month, and under 10% after six months. Service quality was uneven, and the company could replace a cancelled cleaner only 15 to 20% of the time. Good cleaners left to work for clients directly. Homejoy raised about $40M and closed on 31 July 2015.

**Lesson.** Scaling customer acquisition before retention and unit economics work multiplies the losses.
