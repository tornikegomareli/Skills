**Decision.** Steve Jobs returned as interim CEO in 1997. Apple sold a dozen Macintosh versions that differed in small specs. Jobs asked which one he should tell his friends to buy, and nobody had a clear answer. He cut about 70% of Apple's hardware and software products, including the Newton. He kept a 2x2 grid: consumer and professional, desktop and portable. That gave four lines: iMac, iBook, Power Macintosh, and PowerBook. About 3,000 people were laid off.

**Outcome.** Apple lost $1.04B in fiscal 1997. In 1998 it made a $309M profit. The 1998 iMac sold about one million units a year.

**Lesson.** A product line that the company cannot explain in one sentence is a line the customer cannot choose from, so cut it to what you can explain.
