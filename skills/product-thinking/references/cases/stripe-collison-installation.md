**Decision.** In Stripe's early days, the Collison brothers did not send a signup link to a person who agreed to try Stripe. They asked for the person's laptop and set Stripe up for them on the spot. The practice was common enough at Y Combinator to get a name: the "Collison installation". Stripe ran a long private beta before its public launch in September 2011.

**Outcome.** Paul Graham uses it as the standard example of founders who recruit users actively. Stripe later grew to a $159B valuation (February 2026) and processed $1.9 trillion in 2025.

**Lesson.** For early users, remove the setup step yourself instead of hoping they finish it alone.
