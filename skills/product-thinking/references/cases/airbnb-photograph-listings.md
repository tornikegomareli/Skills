**Decision.** Airbnb made about $200 a week and was stuck. At Y Combinator, Paul Graham looked at the New York listings with the founders. The photos were poor. The founders flew to New York, rented a camera, and photographed hosts' homes themselves. They also went door to door to recruit hosts and help them improve their listings. They did this by hand, not with code.

**Outcome.** One week later, weekly revenue doubled to $400. It was the first improvement in more than eight months. Joe Gebbia later said this was the first time someone gave them permission to do things that don't scale.

**Lesson.** When the product is stuck, do the manual work with real users first, and automate it only after it proves its value.
