**Decision.** Burbn was an HTML5 check-in app with four tabs: check-ins, plans, and sharing. Users rarely checked in. They did share photos. Kevin Systrom and Mike Krieger dropped Burbn and started over with one job: share a photo, fast. They prototyped for several months and added filters. They launched Instagram on iPhone on 6 October 2010.

**Outcome.** Instagram had 25,000 users on day one and 1M within about three months. It had 27M by March 2012. Facebook bought it for $1B in April 2012, when it had no revenue and about a dozen employees.

**Lesson.** Find the one thing users already do with your product, cut the rest, and rebuild around it.
