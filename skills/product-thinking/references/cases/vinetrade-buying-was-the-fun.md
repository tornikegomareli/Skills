**Decision.** Vinetrade built an online marketplace for fine wine in the UK to cut out middlemen. It listed stock at good prices and made buying easy. It made trades anonymous and acted as escrow to stop buyers and sellers from going around it.

**Outcome.** People used the site once or twice, then went back to email and phone calls. For these buyers, the talk around a purchase was part of the fun. In a small market where everyone knew each other, cutting out the fee was easy. Warehouse errors forced manual work. Trades reached hundreds of thousands of pounds, but the founder judged it would not scale, and he closed it in 2013.

**Lesson.** If the existing process is part of what the customer enjoys, removing it removes the reason to come back.
