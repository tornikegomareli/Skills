**Decision.** Kite built an AI code-completion tool from 2014. The founder put the work in this order: team, then product, then distribution, then monetization. It gave the product to individual developers first, and most features stayed in the free tier.

**Outcome.** Kite reached product-market fit only in 2019, five years after the start. It grew to 500,000 monthly active developers with almost no marketing spend. Those developers would not pay. Engineering managers did not want to pay for a claimed 18% speed gain. The team stopped work in 2021 and announced the shutdown in November 2022.

**Lesson.** Free users who like a tool do not prove that anyone will pay, so test who pays before you scale distribution.
