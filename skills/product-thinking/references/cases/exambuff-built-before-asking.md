**Decision.** Exambuff let students upload handwritten exam answers for PhD students to mark. The founder ran a survey but did not interview students in person. He coded the most complex version first, about 30,000 lines over 6 months, with a custom image annotator. He waited a year for his university to adopt it before he launched on campus.

**Outcome.** Flyers at another university brought a few visits and no sales. Facebook ads brought a few hundred clicks and no sales. The university said no after a year. The market was only active at exam time. After about two years with no paying customer, the founder stopped.

**Lesson.** Test demand by hand with real buyers before you write code, and avoid a first buyer whose decision takes longer than your runway.
