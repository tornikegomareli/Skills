**Decision.** File sync across platforms is hard to build and hard to explain. Drew Houston tested demand with a video before the product was finished. In April 2007 he posted a 3-minute screencast on Hacker News with his YC application. In March 2008 he made a second video for the Digg audience, with in-jokes for that community.

**Outcome.** The second video got about 12,000 Diggs. The beta waiting list went from 5,000 to 75,000 in one day. At the September 2008 public launch Dropbox had 100,000 registered users. It had 4M users by January 2010.

**Lesson.** If the product is expensive to build, show it to the target users first and count how many ask for it.
